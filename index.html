How it works

Two ways to prove you're human.
One standard for privacy.

Secureye adapts to your device. Front camera on mobile? Iris liveness check. Laptop with no camera? Keystroke pattern analysis. Both are instant. Both leave no data behind.

01 / DETECT
📱

Device detection

Secureye checks your device capabilities in milliseconds. Camera available? Eye verification. Keyboard only? Typing rhythm. Always the fastest path for your hardware.

02 / VERIFY
👁

On-device analysis

Your camera feed or keystrokes are processed entirely in your browser. A lightweight model checks for liveness and human patterns. Nothing is uploaded, streamed, or logged.

03 / PROVE
🔐

Cryptographic token

A signed, single-use token is generated — valid for 60 seconds, tied to the requesting site. The site validates it server-side. Then it expires, permanently.

04 / FORGET
🗑

Nothing stored

No profile. No history. No biometric database. Each verification is completely independent of each other. Our architecture is intentionally designed to be incapable of building a record on you, we hate databases

🧠

Inference on-device, always

The ML model runs inside your browser via WebAssembly. Your camera feed is never sent to a server. We receive a boolean: human or not.

⏱

Tokens expire in 60 seconds

Single-use, time-bound, site-scoped. Even if someone intercepted your token, it's useless in under a minute and tied to one domain.

🚫

No accounts. No cookies. No tracking.

We don't know who you are. We don't want to. Each verification is a clean slate — architecturally impossible to link to any other session.

🔓

Open to audit

The client-side verification code is open source. You can inspect exactly what runs in your browser before trusting it with your camera.

For developers

Two lines of HTML.
That's the integration.

No SDK to install. No backend to configure first. Drop the script, add the div, handle the token. You're done.

HTML
<!-- 1. Load Secureye -->
<script src="https://cdn.secureye.io/secureye.js" defer ></script>

<!-- 2. Place the widget -->
<div class="secureye-widget" data-sitekey="YOUR_KEY" data-callback="onVerified" ></div>

<!-- 3. Handle the token -->
<script>
function onVerified(token) {
// token is single-use, expires in 60s
submitForm(token);
}
</script>
